// faq

Questions, answered

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After you describe your project, you receive a written quote with the scope, timeline and price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

How does payment work?

Payment terms (amounts, schedule and method) are agreed in writing in each proposal before work begins. This website does not take payments.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Every delivery includes free revisions within 14 days of delivery for anything inside the agreed scope. Just tell me what needs to change.

Do you work with clients outside Argentina?

Yes. I work remotely, communicate in Spanish or English, and am based in Buenos Aires (Argentina Time, UTC−3).

Can you join an existing project?

Yes. I can add features, fix issues or integrate AI into an app or platform you already have, as well as build new products from scratch.

Who will I be working with?

Me, directly. Uzquiano Labs is my freelance practice, so you talk to the engineer doing the work.
