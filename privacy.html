What this website collects

This website does not have user accounts, does not store form submissions, does not process payments and does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

  • Contact form: submitting the form only opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent to or stored by this website. I receive your details only if you choose to send that email.
  • Theme preference: if you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Third-party services

Emails you send me

If you email or call me, I use your name, contact details and project information only to reply to you and, if we work together, to deliver the project and issue invoices. I do not sell or share this information for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you by emailing uzquianohernan02@gmail.com. Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326) you may also contact the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública, the authority responsible for enforcing it.

Changes
